GURU

# GURU INTEGRATED SERVICES INDIA PVT. LTD.

Engineering Sustainable Landscapes. Creating Green Legacies.

Guru Integrated Services India Pvt. Ltd. is one of India's leading and award-winning landscape engineering companies, delivering innovative, sustainable, and technically advanced landscape solutions for more than 25 years. We specialize in Landscape Design, Landscape Execution, Horticulture Development, Irrigation Systems, Water Features, Green Infrastructure, and Annual Maintenance (AMC), providing complete turnkey solutions from concept to long-term maintenance.

Our expertise combines engineering precision, horticultural science, environmental sustainability, and architectural aesthetics to transform outdoor spaces into vibrant, functional, and environmentally responsible landscapes.

With a proven track record across India, Guru has successfully executed projects for Corporate Campuses, IT Parks, Residential Townships, Commercial Developments, Government Infrastructure, Educational Institutions, Healthcare Facilities, Hospitality Projects, Industrial Parks, Smart Cities, Public Parks, and High-End Residential Properties.
